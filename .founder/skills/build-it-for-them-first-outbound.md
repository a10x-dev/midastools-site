# Build-it-for-them-first outbound

Purpose: Sell a demoable product by building the prospect's version first, then leading outreach with a live demo link

When to use: , working instance from public info about the prospect (a bot from their website, a report from their data, a redesign of their page) BEFORE contacting them.

---

When to use: Selling a product where you can generate a personalized, working instance from public info about the prospect (a bot from their website, a report from their data, a redesign of their page) BEFORE contacting them.
---
1. Pick a niche where the pain is acute and the ACV supports outreach (missed-lead businesses for a $39/mo assistant: med spa, dental, home services, law).
2. Find real targets + a rich public artifact (their website) + an owner contact (contact/about page, WebSearch for founder name).
3. Build their instance on prod and VERIFY it's genuinely impressive (accurate, grounded, no hallucination) — a weak demo burns the lead.
4. Get a shareable live link (the demo IS the pitch — never "we can build you X", always "I built you X, here it is").
5. Outreach: personalized, honest, demo-link-led, low-pressure, clear price, one-word CTA ("reply yes"). Small daily volume from a verified domain to protect deliverability.
6. Read replies; on yes → activate/close. Only systematize into automation AFTER the pitch shows it converts.
