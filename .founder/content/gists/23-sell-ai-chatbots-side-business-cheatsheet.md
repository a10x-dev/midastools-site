## Common mistakes

1. **Pitching before you build the demo.** The demo *is* the pitch. Never send a sales email without the live link.
2. **Building from a thin website.** If the business's site has no hours/services/pricing, the bot has little to answer from — pick businesses with real content, or add a few FAQs when you build.
3. **Underpricing.** $50/mo signals "cheap." $300/mo signals "this protects real revenue." Anchor to the value of *one* recovered lead.
4. **Selling "a chatbot" instead of "never missing a lead."** Owners don't want software; they want the booked appointment that would've gone to a competitor.
5. **Chasing 50 niches at once.** Pick ONE (dental *or* med spa *or* HVAC), get 5 clients, then expand. Ten businesses in one metro is a real recurring business.
